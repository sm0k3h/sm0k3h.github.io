Proper Usage

Whiteout Survival Helper bot demonstrates everything shown in /help command. You may use these commands as you would like within your server as long as what you are doing does not break discord's ToS.

Whiteout Survival Helper DOES NOT store GuildIDs and UserID. You can also join our support server "WoS Frostfire" by typing "/support" to get the Discord servers links.

Access to Data

Access to Data is only permitted to Bot's developer, and only in the scope required for the development, testing, and implementation of features for Bot. Data is not sold, provided to, or shared with any third party.

User Rights

At any time, you have the right to request to view the Data pertaining to your Discord account. You may submit a request through WoS FrostFire Server. You have the right to request the removal of relevant Data. You can view the data upon request from @popodood.

Underage Users

The use of the Bot is not permitted for minors under the age of 13, or under the age of legal consent for their country. This is in compliance with the Discord Terms of Service. No information will be knowingly stored from an underage user. If it is found out that a user is underage we will take all necessary action to delete the stored data.

Questions

If you have any questions or are concerned about what data might be being stored from your account contact @popodood, or join either "WoS FrostFire". For more information check the Discord Terms Of Service.
